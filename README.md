## ✨ Features

* 🎵 A **black circular vinyl disc** with album artwork in the center
* 🐱 Cute **cat album cover** displayed on the record
* ▶️ When you press **Play**, the vinyl spins like a real record player
* 🧩 **Checkered gray-and-white background** for a vintage, cozy feel

---

## 🎛️ Player Details

* Displays the **song date and title** (`date`)
* Shows the **artist name** (`frad`)
* Indicates playlist status: **“Playing 1 of 9”**
* Includes a **progress bar** showing:

  * Current time: `0:10`
  * Total duration: `3:51`

---

## ⏮️⏯️⏭️ Controls

The player uses **three simple green buttons**:

* **Back** – go to the previous song
* **Play / Pause** – start or stop the music
* **Forward** – skip to the next song

---

## 🎼 Playlist

* Contains **9 songs**
* Songs can be navigated using the control buttons

---

## 🌱 Purpose

This project combines the **nostalgic feel of vinyl records** with **simple, modern controls**.
It is designed as a learning project for me as a beginner who wants to practice building a music player while improving my coding skills.
